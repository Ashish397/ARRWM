fit_pca_basis.py          motion corpus -> PCA basis     400k frames
    component directions |cos| >= 0.9985 over the top 8
    PC0 (throttle) and PC1 (steer) to 0.99999
    mean differs: the shipped basis was fitted on a stationary-weighted sample.
    It sets only the origin of the action space and is shared by conditioning,
    critic target and eval read-back, so it cancels in every
    commanded-vs-realized relationship the paper reports.
